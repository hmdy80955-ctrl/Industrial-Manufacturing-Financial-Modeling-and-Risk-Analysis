# 🏭 Industrial Manufacturing Financial Modeling & Logistics Risk Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive, dynamic financial model tailored for a large-scale industrial manufacturing enterprise. It simulates the end-to-end financial workflows of procuring core raw materials (Copper, Aluminum, and Heavy Machinery Components), calculating precise **Landed Costs**, structuring a multi-tiered **Income Statement**, and conducting dynamic **What-If Scenario Analysis** to evaluate macroeconomic supply chain risks.

## 🎯 3. Financial Performance Analytics & KPIs

* **Gross Profit Margin:** **33%** — Demonstrates strong core manufacturing pricing power and raw material efficiency.
* **Net Profit Margin:** **18%** — Reflects robust bottom-line operational efficiency even after absorbing distribution and tax burdens.
* **Logistics & Customs to COGS Ratio:** **11%** — A vital metric for the supply chain division, isolating total freight and import tariff stress against total manufacturing costs.

## 🔍 4. C-Suite Strategic Insights (What-If Risk Assessment)
1. **Supply Chain Resilience:** This model successfully pressure-tested a macroeconomic stress scenario where global Copper prices surged by 27.7% and ocean freight costs escalated. 
2. **Bottom-Line Impact:** While the automated framework absorbed a **23% contraction in Net Profit**, the enterprise remained highly profitable with an 18% net margin, proving structural financial stability.
3. **Strategic Mitigation:** It is highly recommended that the procurement division locks in long-term hedging contracts for core metals, and evaluates a partial cost-pass-through pricing model to safeguard the historical 23% Net Profit baseline.
